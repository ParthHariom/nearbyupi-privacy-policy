NearbyUPI

Privacy Policy

How the NearbyUPI Android app handles your information — in plain language.

Effective date: 12 November 2026

1What NearbyUPI is

NearbyUPI is an Android app made by PayPulse Labs. It lets you share your UPI ID with people standing near you over Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE), so they can pay you without scanning a QR code or typing your ID. Payments themselves are always processed by your UPI app (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, etc.) and your bank — NearbyUPI never touches your money or your bank account.

2What we NEVER collect

  • No bank passwords, UPI PINs or card details — ever.
  • No phone contacts — we never read your address book.
  • No SMS or call logs — no SMS-permission is requested.
  • No precise location / GPS — the app only uses Bluetooth; it never records where you are.
  • No microphone or camera — the QR feature only shows your own QR; it does not scan other people's.
  • No phone number or IMEI.

3Information you provide

  • Your UPI ID (e.g. name@oksbi) — you type this in yourself. It is stored only on your device and is encrypted before it is broadcast over Bluetooth. If you enable cloud backup (see section 6), it is also stored in our Firebase database so your ID can be restored.
  • A display name (optional) — shown with your broadcast so payees can recognise you.
  • Payment history (optional) — records of payments you make (payee, amount, status) are saved on your device so you can see your history. If cloud backup is enabled, these records sync to our Firebase database tied to an anonymous device identifier — not your email or phone number.

4How the Bluetooth broadcast works

When you turn the broadcast on, your encrypted UPI ID travels over Bluetooth Low Energy directly between phones — it is not sent through our servers or the internet. Only devices within roughly 10–30 metres that also have the app open can receive it. Turning the broadcast off stops transmission immediately. Anyone who receives the payload only learns your UPI ID and display name — nothing else about you.

5Permissions the app requests

  • Nearby devices (Bluetooth) — required to discover nearby payers and broadcast your UPI ID. The core feature.
  • Notifications — used for the "broadcast active" service notification and the Bluetooth-off reminder. You can deny this; the app still works.
  • Unrestricted battery (optional) — a Settings shortcut so Android doesn't kill background Bluetooth on some phones.

That's the complete list. NearbyUPI requests no location, no contacts, no storage, no camera, no microphone permissions.

6Cloud backup (optional)

If you turn on cloud backup, your UPI ID and payment history are stored in Google Firebase (Firestore) under a randomly generated anonymous device ID. This lets your data survive app reinstalls and device changes. You can email us any time to have it deleted (see section 9). If you never enable backup, nothing leaves your phone — everything stays in the app's local storage.

7Third-party services

We use a small number of trusted Google services inside the app:

  • Google AdMob (ads) — shows banner ads. AdMob may collect device identifiers and approximate data to serve and measure ads. Read Google's own policy: policies.google.com/technologies/ads.
  • Google Play services (in-app review) — the Play Store rating prompt. Handled entirely by Google.
  • Google Firebase — anonymous authentication and Firestore storage, only if cloud backup is enabled.

These providers process data under their own privacy policies. We do not sell your data to anyone.

8Data retention & deletion

  • Uninstalling the app deletes all locally stored data (UPI ID, history, preferences) immediately.
  • Cloud backup — email us and we will delete your backed-up records from Firestore, or you can simply disable backup in the app.
